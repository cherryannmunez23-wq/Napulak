Discover Mt. Napulak

Mt. Napulak is a beautiful mountain located in Igbaras, Iloilo, Philippines. It is known for its unique rocky summit, green mountain slopes, and breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape. Many hikers and nature lovers visit Mt. Napulak to enjoy hiking, explore mountain trails, and experience the beauty of nature. Its amazing scenery makes it a wonderful place for taking pictures and enjoying an outdoor adventure.

Visitors are encouraged to keep the mountain clean, respect the plants and animals, and protect its natural beauty for future generations.
